# 📚 @notebooklm

## 📌 Overview
- **Trigger**: `@notebooklm`
- **Category**: For Students (Study, Writing & Office Productivity)
- **Purpose**: Synthesize, summarize, and query academic sources, study guides, and multiple complex documents using advanced synthesis techniques.
- **Best For**: Studying for midterms/exams, writing comprehensive literature reviews, and synthesizing lecture notes.

---

## 🚀 How to Trigger
In your Promptimity AI chat, type:
```text
@notebooklm [paste text from notes/sources, or describe the documents you want to query]
```

---

## 💡 Key Capabilities & Features

- **Multi-source Synthesis**: Combines information from separate class notes, slide transcripts, and textbook chapters into cohesive, structured summaries.
- **Study Guide Generation**: Automatically compiles custom study guides, vocabulary flashcards, and practice quiz questions based on raw source material.
- **Concept Deep-Diving**: Explains difficult academic terms, equations, or philosophical arguments in multiple formats (e.g. analogical, technical, simplified).
- **Source Citation Mapping**: Traces facts and statements back to the specific uploaded document or input note to ensure accuracy.
- **Audio Podcast Outlining**: Outlines engaging, two-way podcast dialogues explaining your source material (similar to NotebookLM's audio overview).

## 🛠️ Real-world Examples

### Example 1: Study Guide Creation
**Prompt:**
> `@notebooklm I have pasted our biology lecture notes on cell division and photosynthesis below. Let's summarize the key cycles, create a glossary of terms, and draft 10 practice multiple-choice questions.`

### Example 2: Synthesizing Research Papers
**Prompt:**
> `@notebooklm here are executive summaries of three different research papers on renewable energy batteries. Synthesize their findings, compare their efficiency metrics in a markdown table, and list the open challenges they all note.`

---

## 📘 Best Practices
1. **Clearly Label Sources**: When pasting text from multiple sources, demarcate them with simple headers (e.g. `[Source 1: Lecture Notes]`) to help the AI map citations accurately.
2. **Verify Important Dates/Math**: Always cross-reference crucial dates, mathematical constants, or historical facts with your official course syllabi.
3. **Ask for Clarification**: If a summary contains a highly complex explanation, ask the AI to "explain it to me like I am a 12-year-old" or "give me an analogy."
