# 📚 @doc-coauthoring

## 📌 Overview
- **Trigger**: `@doc-coauthoring`
- **Category**: For Students (Study, Writing & Office Productivity)
- **Purpose**: Collaborate with the AI to draft research papers, essay structures, lab reports, or technical documentations.
- **Best For**: Creative writing, structured essays, group projects, and setting up clean report frameworks.

---

## 🚀 How to Trigger
In your Promptimity AI chat, type:
```text
@doc-coauthoring [paste your current draft, outline, or describe the paper/report you need to co-write]
```

---

## 💡 Key Capabilities & Features

- **Cohesive Outline Drafting**: Drafts structured outlines (e.g. Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results, Conclusion) with thematic targets for each section.
- **Tone & Voice Adjustment**: Refines academic papers for formal styles, technical documentations for clear execution guides, or creative pieces for vivid narratives.
- **Paragraph Transition Flow**: Connects disjointed arguments or sections with elegant transition statements to improve readability.
- **Citation & Referencing Frameworks**: Structures bibliographies, footnotes, and inline citations in standard academic formats (APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE).
- **Prose Polishing & Proofreading**: Identifies passive voice, wordy sentences, repetitive vocabulary, and grammar issues, proposing clean alternatives.

## 📘 Best Practices
1. **Maintain Original Intent**: Ensure co-authored sections preserve your original research findings, arguments, and conclusions without speculative additions.
2. **Review Formatting Guides**: Check your school's or publisher's exact guidelines for page sizes, font constraints, and reference layouts.
3. **Draft Incrementally**: Do not attempt to co-write an entire 10-page paper in one prompt. Focus on one section or argument at a time for the best results.
