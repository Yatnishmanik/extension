# 📚 @docx

## 📌 Overview
- **Trigger**: `@docx`
- **Category**: For Students (Study, Writing & Office Productivity)
- **Purpose**: Create, edit, format, and structure Microsoft Word (.docx) documents, or collaborate on written reports and essays.
- **Best For**: Formatting homework assignments, drafting formal letters, compiling term papers, and managing document templates.

---

## 💡 Key Capabilities & Features

- **Document Hierarchy Design**: Outlines clear heading structures (Heading 1, Heading 2, Body Text) to ensure readable, accessible, and easily navigable Word documents.
- **Python-docx Automation**: Generates complete, functional Python scripts using the `python-docx` library to programmatically compile, format, and edit Word files.
- **Typography & Style Guidelines**: Proposes elegant typography pairings, paragraph spacing, line heights, page margins, and header/footer configurations.
- **Lists and Callouts Formatting**: Formulates clean bullet points, numbered lists, nested lists, and visually distinct blockquotes or tip boxes.
- **Report Templates**: Builds standard layouts for lab reports, business proposals, meeting minutes, and academic research papers.

## 📘 Best Practices
1. **Use Paragraph Styles**: When building Word documents, always use standard Paragraph Styles (e.g., 'Normal', 'Heading 1') instead of applying local inline font changes, as this keeps the file modular.
2. **Set Consistent Spacing**: Maintain a uniform double-space or 1.15-space layout with 6pt/12pt spacing after paragraphs rather than inserting blank double enters.
3. **Verify Table Cells**: Ensure all programmatically generated tables specify precise cell widths and have proper cell padding to avoid text clipping.
