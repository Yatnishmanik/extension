# 📚 @pptx

## 📌 Overview
- **Trigger**: `@pptx`
- **Category**: For Students (Study, Writing & Office Productivity)
- **Purpose**: Build beautifully styled PowerPoint slide decks and presentations.
- **Best For**: School presentations, project pitches, thesis defenses, and formal office slides.

---

## 🚀 How to Trigger
In your Promptimity AI chat, type:
```text

```

---

## 💡 Key Capabilities & Features

- **Slide Outline & Narrative Flow**: Organizes presentation structures to capture attention, balance text density, and flow logically from problem statement to final summary.
- **Python-pptx Slide Generator**: Outputs robust Python scripts using the `python-pptx` library to programmatically assemble clean slides with custom positioning.
- **Visual Design Systems**: Proposes stunning slide color palettes (Harmonious contrast), clean typography pairs, and rules for white space balance.
- **Data Callout & Chart Layouts**: Details elegant placements for key metrics, comparison grids, and graphical representations.
- **Speaker Notes Drafting**: Co-writes engaging speaker notes corresponding to each slide to help you present with confidence.

## 📘 Best Practices
1. **Rule of 6x6**: Avoid packing slides with paragraphs. Limit slide content to a maximum of 6 bullet points, with no more than 6 words per bullet point.
2. **High Visual Contrast**: Ensure slide backgrounds and text colors have high contrast (e.g. crisp white text on deep charcoal background, or charcoal text on off-white).
3. **Use Consistent Templates**: Keep margins, font sizes, heading weights, and logo coordinates uniform across all slides for a professional polish.
